# Jeppaheimur — website preview

A working preview of the Jeppaheimur site — aukahlutir í jeppann / accessories for 4x4s.

## This is a draft, not the finished shop

- **Every image and the hero video are temporary stand-ins.** All of them are meant to be
  replaced by Edvard's own photographs — that is the single biggest improvement available.
- **All prices, product names and stock numbers are examples**, not real ones.
- **The figures on Sendingar (Shipping) and Heildsala (Wholesale) are placeholders** —
  free-shipping threshold, delivery time, returns period, minimum wholesale order.
  These have to come from Edvard before anything goes live.
- The catalogue is a small sample, just enough to see the pages working.

## What to try

- **Veldu bíl / Choose car** — pick a make and see only the parts that fit it
- **Veldu hlut / Choose part** — the whole catalogue, filterable by make and category
- **IS / EN** — the language switch in the header is live
- **Heildsala · Sagan okkar · Spurt og svarað · Hafa samband** — all working pages

Comments on structure, wording and look are all welcome.
